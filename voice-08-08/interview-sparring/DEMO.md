# 60-second live demo

## Before the clock

Run `npm start`, open <http://127.0.0.1:4173>, allow microphone access, and
leave the page at the hero. Use speakers or headphones so the judge can hear the
Realtime interviewer.

## Live human exchange

**0:00–0:06 — Start**

Say: “Rehearsed answers evaporate under the first human follow-up. This one
pushes back while I am still under pressure.” Click **Start live round**. The
OpenAI Realtime voice asks the first banked question.

**0:06–0:25 — Deliberately omit the action**

When asked about leading through uncertainty, answer naturally:

> “The situation was an uncertain billing migration across three teams. My task
> was to keep the launch safe. The project shipped on schedule and support
> tickets fell twenty-eight percent.”

Do not say what you personally did. The deterministic STAR rule should mark
Action missing and hand the voice this press: “What did you personally decide
or do—not the team?”

**0:25–0:38 — Answer the live pushback**

Reply:

> “I personally created the rollback checklist and led a daily risk review.”

Point to **MISSING ACTION → FOLLOW-UP SELECTED IN CODE** and the newly lit
Action signal. Say: “The voice phrased and delivered that in real time. Code
selected why and what to press.”

**0:38–0:50 — Prove the pressure is voice-native**

Let the next question begin, then say: “Typing would let me silently rewrite
the answer. Here I have to respond at conversational speed, and a 45-second
timer can cut me off. That timing is the product.” End the live round.

**0:50–1:00 — Show what the buyer gets**

Click **Watch 3-question replay** and, once it finishes, point to the computed
debrief: per-question scores, time-budget graph, worst dodge quoted verbatim,
and two questions to rerun. Say: “The buyer is an active job seeker. The store
category is Education / Careers. The first paid version is **$14.99 a month
during the job-search spike**.”

The replay is fixture-driven through the same state machine and metric code. It
is the judging-day fallback if microphone permission or the network fails, not
the primary demo.
